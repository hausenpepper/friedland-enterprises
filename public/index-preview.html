Approach

How we engage. Three steps, by name, owned at handoff.

01 Engage

Operators, not consultants.

Engagements are staffed by people who have run the function inside companies at scale. The principal sits in every engagement. The right bench members come in for the work that needs them. We bring people by name, not by title. Meet the bench.

02 Deploy

Output the operator owns.

Sometimes the work is decision support: a market read, a strategy memo, a board document. Sometimes it's operational change: a deployed system, a re-engineered workflow, a hired team. We agree on which up front, and ship something the operator owns at handoff.

03 Exit

Crawl, walk, run.

Every engagement starts with one workflow we can ship in weeks. We expand only after value is proven inside the business. We exit when the operator's team owns the work and the systems behind it. Concentrated time on a small number of operators each year.
