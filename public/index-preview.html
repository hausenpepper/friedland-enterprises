01  What We Do

Investments

Investments are the firm's identity and the underwriting credential for everything else. Three programs run in parallel: concentrated activist positions in microcap public equity, board investments in private mid-market operating companies, and a California real estate partnership.

Activist public-equity engagement

We hold concentrated positions in select microcap public companies where disclosure, operating focus, or capital allocation can be materially improved. Our framework, internally called Type B, screens for microcap companies with positive gross profit and what we describe as opacity discounts: businesses the market underprices because management has not made the operating story legible.

Our engagement is constructive and KPI-driven. We publish operating theses, build public scoreboards against the metrics that matter, and hold positions long enough for the work to matter.

Current and prior engagements: CMRC, TRVG, LVLU, GRWG

Private-company board investments

We hold board seats and operating-investor positions in private mid-market companies across building products, hardware and security, hydroponics and specialty distribution, and surfaces and flooring.

04  What We Do

Selective operating partnerships

The systems we deploy in our own operating companies, and the operating questions we work on inside our private boards, are increasingly built on AI. Each year we partner with two or three outside operating companies to deploy that same playbook. We do not run a consulting practice in the traditional sense. We accept partnerships only where we understand the industry, where the operator is committed to moving, and where we can ship a working system inside a defined window.

AI in operations

Workflow automation in operations, customer service, and procurement. Configured for the operator's actual stack and decision points, not a reference architecture.

Finance ops automation

Automation across AP, AR, cash forecasting, and management reporting. Systems your finance team operates, not a dashboard that depends on us.

Commerce and supply chain AI

Repricing, catalog management, merchandising, and inventory intelligence. The category we have spent two decades operating in.

Engagements run eight to sixteen weeks and end with a working system the operator's team owns.

How We Work

Observe. Analyze. Activate.

We do not start with recommendations. We start by understanding how the business actually runs.

01  Observe

Observe

We map operations: people, workflows, tools, decision points. We identify where human time is being spent on work that a well-configured AI system could handle without losing judgment quality.

02  Analyze

Analyze

We build a crawl-walk-run roadmap. What gets implemented first (highest ROI, lowest disruption), what comes next, what to skip. Every recommendation carries a cost estimate and a success metric.

03  Activate

Activate

We deploy. Tool selection, configuration, integration with your stack, training your team, and staying involved until the system is stable. We bring in specialists from our operating network where needed.

Most engagements run eight to sixteen weeks. The deliverable is a working system, not a slide deck.
